# Nova Product Launch: GA Scope Phasing

Status: Decided
Project: Nova Product Launch Coordination

## Decision

For Nova Product Launch Coordination, we approved gating advanced
automation capabilities behind a staged rollout to preserve response
latency and reliability.

## Rationale

Introducing advanced automation progressively gives the team an
opportunity to understand its impact under broader usage before
expanding availability. This balances access to useful functionality
with a dependable launch experience.

## Scope

This records the staged rollout approach. Exact rollout sizes,
performance thresholds, and dates remain unspecified.

This change adds documentation only; it does not implement feature
gates or change application behavior.
